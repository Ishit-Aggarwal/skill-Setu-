## 1. Your role and goal

You are an experienced scriptwriter for product demo videos who has watched many Smart India Hackathon (SIH) and Devpost-winning demos. Write the **complete narration script and shot list** for our SIH 2026 demo video of **Skill Setu**.

The video is **me (a team member) pitching Skill Setu to someone who has never seen it.** It should sound like a friendly founder showing a new user around: warm, confident and clear. **It must not sound like an engineer explaining code.** The SIH judges are the audience, but you should write as if you are talking to one first-time user: a BAMS student, a professor, a recruiter at an Ayurveda company, or a placement officer.

## 2. Hard rules (do not break any of these)

1. **Total runtime is 5:00 or less.** Aim for **4:40 to 4:50** so there is a safety margin. Put a running timestamp on every scene.
2. **Word budget:** spoken narration of **600 to 660 words** in total. At a natural pace of about 140 words per minute, that leaves room for pauses while the screen shows something. Give the word count for each scene and the grand total at the end.
3. **The narration is read by me, a human team member.** SIH 2026 rules say the demo video and its audio narration must **not** be AI-generated and must be delivered by team members. So write for a real person to read aloud: short sentences, natural breathing points, no tongue-twisters, and nothing that only works in a robotic voice.
4. **The visuals are a real screen recording of the working site,** not AI-generated video and not mock-ups. Every on-screen action you write must be something that can actually be clicked in Skill Setu (see the feature inventory in section 6). **Do not invent features, numbers, partners or users.**
5. **No technical jargon in the narration.** Never say: Next.js, React, Convex, database, backend, API, JSON, schema, MediaPipe, YAMNet, Gemini, LLM, model, embeddings, Jaccard, cron, state machine, OTP token or deployment. Use the translations in section 8. (A small, quiet on-screen caption like "AI-powered" is fine.)
6. **Highlight the AI features, but don't make the video only about them.** AI features get about **40 to 45 percent** of the runtime (roughly 2 minutes). Everything else still gets shown, so the judges see a complete platform for all four roles.
7. **Be honest about what the AI does.** Only call something "AI" if it is marked ★ in section 6. The internship "skill match %", the skill-gap heatmaps and the curriculum-alignment report come from students' real test scores and live job postings. Describe them as "smart", "data-driven" or "built from real results", **not** as AI. Judges catch overclaiming.
8. **Repeat the same trust message throughout:** *"a resume says it, Skill Setu proves it."* The core idea is **verified skills**, meaning proctored tests leading to certificates that anyone can check. Every AI feature should connect back to it.

## 3. The problem we are solving (use it for the hook; keep it human)

- SIH 2026 problem statement **SIH26044**, from the **Ministry of AYUSH** (theme: Smart Automation): *"Portal for Academia–Industry collaboration for Skill Mapping, Internships and Placement."*
- AYUSH covers five systems of medicine: **Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy**. Students study BAMS, BHMS, BUMS, BSMS and BNYS.
- Scale, for context: about **52,000 AYUSH seats across roughly 900 colleges** (2025 figures from counselling sources). If you use a number on screen, round it and say "over 50,000". Do not invent any other statistics.
- The pain, told as a story:
  - **Students** graduate with marks, not proof of practical skill. Their resume says "Panchakarma, pharmacovigilance, GMP" and nobody can check it.
  - **Industry** (Ayurveda pharma companies, AYUSH hospitals, research councils, wellness companies) can't tell who is actually job-ready, so hiring is slow and depends on guesswork.
  - **Professors** spend hours writing test papers by hand and have no clear view of what employers want.
  - **Colleges** (placement cells) track drives, MOUs and placement numbers in spreadsheets and can't see where their students are weakest before accreditation (NAAC/NCISM) reporting.
- **Skill Setu ("setu" means bridge)** is one portal that connects all four, for all five AYUSH systems.

## 4. The story (the spine of the video)

Research on winning hackathon demos gives one clear rule: **a demo is a story, not a feature list.** Follow one student through the whole journey and bring in the other three roles as the story needs them:

> **Aarav Sharma**, a 4th-year BAMS student at the All India Institute of Ayurveda (AIIA), New Delhi, wants an internship. His resume *claims* skills. Skill Setu helps him **prove** them: a professor (**Dr. Shalini Kulkarni**) builds a test from her own notes in minutes, Aarav takes it in a secure exam room, gets a certificate, and a recruiter verifies it in seconds. His college sees exactly where the whole batch needs help.

These are the real demo accounts in the site's **Demo Mode** (the Student, Academician, Industry and Institution personas). Use these names, because all of this data already exists in the demo.

## 6. Complete feature inventory (grounded in the actual site)

★ = genuinely AI-powered. The route in brackets tells the screen recorder where to go.

### Everyone / platform
- **Four workspaces**: Student, Industry, Academician (professor/faculty) and Institution (college placement cell). Each gets its own dashboard.
- **All five AYUSH systems throughout**: every test, posting and profile is tagged Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha or Homoeopathy, and you can filter by them.
- **Demo Mode** on the home page: choose a persona and explore with realistic data. **Reset demo data** in the header restores everything.
- **Verified sign-up**: email verification code, and organisation accounts (industry, faculty, institution) need a verification code.
- **Works on any device**: sign in anywhere and your data follows you.
- **Public certificate check** at `/verify/<code>`: anyone can confirm a certificate is real.
- Notifications bell and inbox, automatic reminders before open-window tests close, and a **Directory** of institutes, mentors and companies.

### Student
- **Dashboard** (`/dashboard/student`): profile strength, a **skill profile** built from real test scores, **Skill Gap Nudges** ("your weakest areas, mapped against industry demand"), internships "based on your skill profile" and upcoming tests.
- ★ **Resume Coach** (`/resume-coach`):
  - The student uploads a resume (or uses their Skill Setu portfolio) and picks a goal: a career track (e.g. "Panchakarma Therapist", "QC/QA Analyst: ASU&H Pharma", "AYUSH Pharmacovigilance Associate") or one specific internship.
  - The AI reads the resume and compares **what it claims with what the student's proctored tests have verified**, shown on a "claimed vs verified" radar. A strong claim with no verified score behind it is flagged **"Claimed, not verified."**
  - It recommends the **next tests to take, chosen only from real tests the student can actually register for** (it cannot invent one).
  - It builds a **week-by-week study plan** that cites real textbooks and syllabus units (e.g. *Charaka Samhita*, *Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India*, NCISM syllabus units). It gives no random web links.
  - It suggests **resume fixes** with before/after examples (missing sections, vague skills, outcomes without numbers).
  - It keeps a history of earlier analyses.
  - Privacy: phone numbers, emails and ID numbers are removed. The student can delete everything at any time, and it is deleted automatically after 180 days.
- **Skill tests** (`/skill-assessment`): browse, register, see sample papers and take tests online in the secure exam room, or sit offline/hybrid tests where the professor records the mark.
- **Internships & jobs** (`/internships`): Panchakarma, clinical, pharma, research and wellness roles. Filter by AYUSH system and more. Each role shows a **skill match %** built from the student's verified scores, plus an eligibility check (course, minimum skill score). Save roles or apply with one click, then track them under **Applied** and **Saved** (`/applications/internships`, `/saved/internships`).
- **Placement Readiness** (`/readiness`): "what a recruiter sees before they read anything else, and exactly what is missing", as a checklist of what the profile unlocks.
- **Portfolio** (`/portfolio`): headline, skills, projects, education and certifications, with proof reviewed by the institution. **Verified certificates** can be featured at the top. There is an "Open to opportunities" toggle and a resume page.
- **Certificates**: download the PDF, share to LinkedIn, and choose whether each one shows on the profile.
- **My analytics** (`/analytics`): you vs your cohort, score trend over time, application funnel and "where to spend your time". Nothing is illustrative; it's all built from real results.
- **Mentorship** (`/mentorship`): find faculty and industry mentors, book sessions and office hours on a calendar, and track applied/saved mentorships.
- **Communities** (`/communities`): join your professor's or college's community to read announcements, download notes and take members-only tests.

### Academician (professor / faculty)
- **Host a test**, with four ways to build the paper:
  1. ★ **Generate with AI**: describe the topic, number of questions and difficulty, and the AI drafts the multiple-choice questions with explanations.
  2. ★ **Generate from my documents**: upload your own syllabus PDF, Word notes, PowerPoint slides, spreadsheets or even a **photo of handwritten topics**. The AI first builds a **topic map** of what the material covers (the professor edits it), then writes questions for the chosen topics. Each question carries a **difficulty, a Bloom's level and a citation chip pointing to where in your notes it came from.** Repeated questions are removed.
  3. ★ **Import an existing paper**: upload your old paper, even split across several files with the answer key in a spreadsheet. Questions are copied exactly as written. Where no answer is given, the AI fills it in and marks it "answer generated, please check."
  4. ★ **Questions from sample papers**: new questions on the **same concepts, never the same question**. Anything too similar to a sample is automatically dropped.
  - Plus **Write manually**.
- ★ **Recheck with AI**: one click checks a question for factual errors, unclear wording or a wrong answer key, and proposes a fix. The professor accepts or keeps the original. **Nothing is published without the professor's review.**
- **Test options**: a fixed sitting, or an **open window** (24 hours to 90 days, so every candidate gets the full time whenever they start). Also question shuffling, dealing each student a different set from a larger pool, the penalty per violation, up to 5 sample papers, the audience (public or community members only), and automatic certificates.
- **Proctoring report** for every candidate: an event timeline, the reference photo taken when the paper opened, and the recording.
- ★ **Certificate designer** (`/certificate-settings`): **the AI designs a certificate** for your institution (colours, border, layout, seal). Press "Regenerate" and you get a genuinely different look, not just a recolour. Or **upload your existing certificate** and the AI recreates it as an editable template. Add your logo and signature.
- **Communities**: Open, Closed or Invite-only, with posts, study materials, links, members-only tests, pinned announcements, moderation (ban with a reason) and an audit log.
- **Faculty Development Programmes** (`/academician/programs`): host FDPs with seats, waitlist, attendance, feedback and certificates.
- **Research collaborations** (`/academician/collabs`): propose projects or respond to them, then run them with milestones, shared documents and logged outputs (papers, chapters, reports).
- **Mentorship** of students, with office hours on a calendar, plus **My students**.
- **Industry Alignment** (`/academician/alignment`): what employers are hiring for vs where *your own students* are weakest, whether the gap is closing, and a suggested intervention.

### Secure exam room (students taking online tests)
- Steps: consent, then camera & microphone check, then fullscreen. An **End test** button is always visible.
- ★ **AI proctoring that runs on the student's own device**. The video and audio are analysed on the student's laptop itself, so the analysis never leaves it:
  - **no face** in front of the camera
  - **more than one face**
  - **looking away** from the screen (head turned or eyes off-screen)
  - **a different person** than the one who started the test (a quiet identity check against the face captured at the start)
  - **voices in the room** (talking or whispering)
- It also catches leaving fullscreen, switching tabs, right-click and developer shortcuts.
- Each violation costs marks, which the professor sets. Too many, or switching the camera off, fails the attempt. The whole sitting is recorded for the professor's report.
- **Instant grading** when the paper is submitted, and a **certificate is issued automatically**. The student gets a notification saying "Certificate issued."

### Industry (recruiters: ASU&H pharma, AYUSH hospitals, research councils, wellness)
- **Post internships/jobs** with eligibility by course (BAMS/BHMS/BUMS/BSMS/BNYS), AYUSH system, required skills and a minimum skill score.
- **Applicant pipeline**: Applied → Shortlisted → Interview → Offer → Hired, with a **skill match** for every applicant, side-by-side candidate comparison and bulk review.
- **Talent Pool** (`/talent-pool`): search *every* student who has opted in, not just the ones who applied. Filter by skill score, skill domain, institution and verified certificates, and save your searches.
- **Offers & Joining** (`/industry/offers`): offer sent, accepted or declined, and joined. Shows the offer-acceptance rate.
- **Verify certificates** (`/verify`): paste one code for an instant **Valid / Not found** plus "View candidate profile", or **bulk-verify up to 50 codes** at once.
- Host skill tests, issue internship/training certificates, manage the hiring team, maintain a company profile and view **hiring analytics** (funnel, time to hire, platform benchmark).

### Institution (college placement cell)
- **Student roster** (`/institution/students`): add students or bulk-import them from a CSV, filter by department and skill-score band, tag students as eligible for a drive and send notices to their inbox.
- **Cohort Skill Gaps** (`/institution/skill-gaps`): a **heatmap** of average test performance by department × skill domain, the semester-over-semester trend, **"where to intervene first"**, and **Export for accreditation**.
- **Curriculum Alignment** (`/institution/curriculum`): live job postings analysed as **industry demand vs what your programmes produce**, with critical gaps and **suggested electives** to close them, plus an export.
- **Placement drives** (`/institution/drives`): schedule drives, invite companies and set eligibility (batches, minimum skill score).
- **MOUs & partnerships** (`/institution/partnerships`): signed MOUs, their scope, renewal-due alerts, the uploaded document and the real hiring activity behind each partner.
- **Notice board** (announcements to students), **analytics** (placement rate, median stipend, top recruiters and multi-year history with CSV import/export), team activity and the institution profile.
- Institutions can also run communities and host tests.

## 7. Research-backed rules for the video (apply all of them)

These come from guides on hackathon demo videos (Devpost, TAIKAI, Hackathon.com) and from SIH 2026 guidance for students:

1. **Hook within the first 5 to 10 seconds** with the problem. Judges review many projects back to back, so the first line decides whether they lean in.
2. **Problem → solution → demo → impact.** Keep problem plus solution to about 40 seconds combined. Most of the time goes to *showing* the product working.
3. **It's a story, not a feature walkthrough.** Keep returning to Aarav. Every feature must answer "so what does this mean for him, his professor, the recruiter or the college?"
4. **Show, don't tell.** If the narration says "in seconds", the screen should show it happening in seconds. Cut or speed up any loading waits in the edit and say so with a small "sped up" caption, which is more honest.
5. **Use a real, working prototype, not mock-ups.** SIH guidance says hardcoded demos get caught and a working demo is the biggest differentiator.
6. **SIH judges expect AI in 2026, and they reward AI that clearly solves the problem statement.** Tie every AI moment to one of the problem statement's goals: skill mapping, internships/placement, faculty-industry connection, curriculum advisory or verifiable credentials.
7. **One idea per scene and one sentence per click.** Don't narrate UI labels ("now I click the button"). Narrate the *benefit* ("she gets a full paper from her own notes").
8. **Use on-screen captions** for the key phrases (hard-of-hearing judges, muted autoplay): 3 to 6 words each, e.g. "Claimed ≠ Verified", "Built from your own notes", "Checked on-device", "Verified in 1 click".
9. **End with impact and a clear call to action,** not "thank you for watching".
10. **Map the story to the SIH evaluation criteria** (novelty, feasibility, impact/benefit, scalability, user experience). Make sure each one is visibly covered at least once. Add a note in the script margin that marks each one.
